# Analytical Questions

The warehouse is designed to answer these questions:

1. What are total sales by month and by year?
2. What are the top 10 and bottom 10 products by sales?
3. How does each branch perform compared to the others?
4. Who are the top customers by purchase value?
5. How do sales growth rates compare with the previous period?
6. Which product categories are the most profitable (not only the highest in sales)?
7. What is the return rate for each product?
8. How many records were corrected or rejected in each data load?
